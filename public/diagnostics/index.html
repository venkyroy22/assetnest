Global DNS propagation is failing. The domain is currently pointing to Hostinger parking nameservers, which are not correctly serving records for your site.
🛡️
Network Blocker
Detected
We've detected that some networks (specifically those with Sophos Firewalls) are intercepting traffic due to the DNS instability, causing the "Not Private" warning.

How to Fix This

1

Update Nameservers

Go to your domain registrar (where you bought the domain) and change the Nameservers to point to your hosting provider (e.g., Netlify or Vercel). The current ns1.dns-parking.com ones are likely the cause of the global failure.

2

Verify DNS Records

Ensure your A record points to the host IP and your CNAME for www is correctly set. Once fixed, it may take 1-2 hours for the connectivity warning to disappear globally.

3

Public Wi-Fi Workaround

If you see the warning on public Wi-Fi, it's often because the Wi-Fi "Login" page is being blocked by HSTS. Try visiting http://neverssl.com to force the login page to appear.

Note for Sophos Users: If your firewall is blocking the site, it may be categorized as "Uncategorized" or "None". You can request a reassessment at Sophos Support.
